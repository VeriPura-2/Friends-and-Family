The Cost
What a Rejection Actually Costs

A port rejection is survivable.
A recall from the supermarket shelf is not.

Tier 1
Port Rejection
Shipment lost
Destroyed or returned at the importer's cost. The full value of the cargo is at risk. Detention fees accumulate daily while it is resolved.
Tier 2
Market Withdrawal
Multiples of cargo value
Product recalled from distribution networks before reaching shelves. Logistics, storage and disposal costs stack on top of the cargo loss.
Tier 3
Retail Recall
€500K-€10M+
Product pulled from supermarket shelves. Retailer penalties, legal liability, brand destruction. Often existential for smaller importers.
A documentation error that clears the port undetected does not disappear. It travels with the product into distribution, onto the shelf, and into the consumer's hands. VeriPura catches it before the shipment moves. That is the only point where all three tiers of document-related risk are eliminated simultaneously.
